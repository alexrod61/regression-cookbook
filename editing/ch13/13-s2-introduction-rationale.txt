## What was preserved
- The draft's core motivational arc: equidispersion as the failing assumption; distinct consequences of over- vs underdispersion for standard errors; GP's single dispersion parameter; nesting enabling a likelihood-ratio test; NB as the overdispersion-only alternative.
- The draft's research-question bullet list (three questions), revised only for terminology and to re-point the bus question to the practice exercises (per the approved single-running-case architecture decision: crabs = running case; 99 B-Line = coding case in the exercises).
- The draft's assumption trio (Independence / Log-linearity / Dispersion flexibility), the gp_dispersion_figure.png graphic, the Mermaid mind-map content, and the "graphical look before formal modelling" pedagogical device.
- Mind-map chapter nodes/order match _quarto.yml (NB 11, ZIP 12, GP 13) and the D-map node labels verbatim.

## What changed
- Structural: Introduction given anchor and subsections; mind map relocated from front matter into the Introduction (finished-Chapter-10 placement), id typo fixed (fig-generalied- -> fig-generalized-), caption rewritten to "explored so far" phrasing.
- Terminology/notation: $\delta$ -> $\theta$ everywhere (Appendix C alignment, lead-author decision); "outcome"/"predictor" cleaned to "response"/"regressor"; "color" prose -> "colour" (code stays `color`); IRR mention removed in favour of expected-count framing; Canadian spelling (colour, modelled).
- The two-case-study Introduction paragraph rewritten: crabs retained as the single running case with explicit continuity from finished Chapter 10's failed diagnostics (residual deviance vs degrees of freedom, mean–variance check); the 99 B-Line underdispersion case is now PREVIEWED as the practice-exercises coding case instead of a second in-chapter workflow. Deletion of the draft's duplicate variance-to-mean arithmetic from the Introduction (it reappears, executed on the canonical training split, in the EDA stage).
- Assumptions bullets upgraded: support/positivity and the concrete mean–variance form Var(Y_i) = mu_i / (1 - theta)^2 made explicit, with theta in (-1, 1).
- Graphical look gained a prose lead-in (what to inspect) per skill.md section 17; caption recast into theta notation with "variance-to-mean ratio" phrasing.
- NEW subsection Documented Use Cases with a five-row sourced table per skill.md section 5.5. Every citation verified during this stage (Consul & Jain 1973 already in bib as @consul1973; four new entries + @bolker2009 from main appended to book/references.bib).

## Why the changes help
- Statistical correctness: Introduction no longer promises a second full in-chapter case the approved architecture does not include; claims about nesting/LRT and support truncation are accurate.
- Pedagogy: readers meet the model on the same crab data whose Chapter 10 diagnostics failed — the strongest possible motivation and exactly the pointer finished Chapter 10 makes to @sec-generalized-poisson.
- Consistency: theta-notation, mind-map placement, and table classes now match finished chapters/appendices; citations are real and checked (skill.md section 28).
