- Results summary table with the three-perspective framing.
- The final synthesizing paragraph states the chapter's bilateral finding plainly: GP does not buy held-out point predictions (same as ch10's conclusion); it buys calibrated uncertainty (predictive zero probabilities and inferential SEs).

- Mirroring Ch10's metric guide with GP analogues makes the two chapters directly comparable row by row.
